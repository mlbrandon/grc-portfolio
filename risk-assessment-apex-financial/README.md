# Risk Assessment: Apex Financial Partners (Fictional Firm)

A cybersecurity risk assessment for a fictional 30-person financial advisory and accounting firm that manages client investment accounts and tax records. Built as a GRC portfolio project.

## Method

1. Identified 15 assets across data, systems, devices, network, people, and third parties
2. Paired each asset with a threat and a firm-specific exposure
3. Scored likelihood and impact on a 1-5 scale (score = likelihood x impact), using a defined scoring key and rating bands
4. Tagged each risk with a NIST CSF 2.0 function and a treatment decision
5. Recommended a safeguard for each risk, citing CIS Controls v8 where it applies (process and physical controls are labeled)
6. Wrote an executive summary of the top five risks and a recommended order of fixes for leadership

## Results

- 15 risks scored: 2 Critical, 8 High, 5 Medium
- Highest risks: email compromise and ransomware affecting the shared drive and backups (score 20 each)
- Top recommendation: make backups recoverable and turn on multi-factor authentication where it is missing
